FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PageQA AI free to use?

Yes! The extension is free and uses your own free-tier API keys from Google AI Studio, Groq, or OpenRouter.

Are my API keys safe?

100%. Your keys are stored locally inside Chrome's private chrome.storage.local and sent directly from your browser to Google / Groq / OpenRouter — never to any third-party server.

What happens if Gemini hits a rate limit?

PageQA AI first tries other available Gemini models automatically. If all Gemini models are busy, it seamlessly switches to your Groq or OpenRouter backup key.

Does it work on Bangla / Banglish explanations?

Yes! In Settings you can pick Banglish, Bangla (বাংলা), or English for explanations while keeping answers matched to the webpage's original language.
